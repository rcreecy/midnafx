MidnaFX 1.14.0 adds experimental **Sunlight shafts** across supported outdoor
scenes. Native sun position, color and visibility drive rays interrupted by
visible opaque scenery. Select **Profile / Remaster**, or the independent Basic
toggle. Advanced lighting strength ranges from 0–25%, defaulting to 10%.
Vanilla disables the feature. Existing saved settings remain intact until a
profile is selected.

The effect follows native weather and daylight fades, excludes Twilight and
underwater/paused contexts, and bypasses missing, behind-camera and off-screen
sources. Native visibility fades smoothly when the sun becomes hidden. A bounded
linear-light composition preserves source alpha and suppresses nearby foreground
veiling. Water, grading, exposure, bloom, LUT and HUD retain their ordering.

The implementation reuses the existing opaque-stage draw and color/depth snapshot
with no extra targets, passes or asset identities. Shafts-only operation skips
AO/bounce compute work. This is a screen-space approximation, not physical
volumetric transport: hidden, off-screen and transparent/cloud occluders cannot
be reconstructed. Camera motion and foliage silhouettes can change the field.
No universal GPU cost or whole-game motion-quality claim is made.

Windows x64 and Intel macOS x86_64 packages are supplied separately. Mac gameplay
and Metal execution remain unverified; support stays best effort through native
builds and portable checks. See [feature and evidence
notes](https://github.com/rcreecy/midnafx/blob/v1.14.0/docs/sunlight-shafts.md).
