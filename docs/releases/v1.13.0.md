MidnaFX 1.13.0 adds experimental **Water scene reflections** on supported enhanced
water. Nearby visible opaque scenery contributes reflected color using animated
water normals. Select **Profile / Remaster**, or enable both Enhanced water and
Water scene reflections in Basic. Vanilla disables them. Existing saved settings
remain intact until a profile is selected. The existing advanced reflection
strength controls scene reflections and their tint fallback together.

The effect reuses the pre-water color/depth snapshot, surface mask and existing
water draw. It adds no asset identities, render targets or passes. Reflected rays
reject invalid geometry, below-water sources, back-facing surfaces and visible
water feedback; opaque banks remain valid when a water fragment is hidden behind
them. Source alpha and existing shoreline, Fresnel, grading, exposure, bloom, LUT
and HUD composition are preserved.

Analytic GPU checks establish reflected donor color and fallback behavior across
depth ordering, clipping, reversed Z and small/odd targets. This is a first-layer
screen-space approximation: hidden, off-screen, sky and translucent scenery are
unavailable. Camera motion and silhouettes can change source visibility. Misses
retain the original tint. Shader work increases on affected water pixels; no
universal frame-rate claim is made.

Windows x64 and Intel macOS x86_64 packages are supplied separately. Mac gameplay
remains unverified and support stays best effort through native builds and
portable checks. See [feature and evidence
notes](https://github.com/rcreecy/midnafx/blob/v1.13.0/docs/water-scene-reflections.md).
