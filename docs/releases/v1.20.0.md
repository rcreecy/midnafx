MidnaFX 1.20.0 upgrades **Adaptive exposure** for steadier scene brightness as
texture details and small highlights move. The existing independent control and
Remaster integration use the upgrade without a new setting or saved-look migration.

The meter uses 4,096 fixed stratified samples and weighted luminance-percentile
bounds. Small bright or dark features have less influence, while large bright
regions still change exposure. Uniform-scene luminance, native night mood,
untrimmed fade rejection, -0.5 to +0.75 stop limits and adaptation speeds remain.

The existing dispatch, exposure history and grading/water composite are reused.
No new target or fullscreen draw is added. Additional samples, histogram atomics
and synchronization cost GPU work; GPU duration and 4K performance remain unmeasured.

Eight new spatial GPU groups pass alongside existing history, fades, bars, color
and alpha checks. A synthetic translating-stripe comparison reduces the first
0.1-second EV-response range from 0.12601 to 0.000069 stops. This is a focused
sampling comparison, not a measured whole-game improvement or FPS result.
Native Ordon toggle, fullscreen resize/return, live reload and normal close pass
with exit zero. Broad camera motion, dungeons, combat and cutscene art quality
remain unqualified.

Windows x64 and Intel macOS x86_64 packages are supplied separately. Mac gameplay
remains unverified and support stays best effort through native builds and
portable checks. No hosted Metal execution is required. See
[feature and evidence notes](https://github.com/rcreecy/midnafx/blob/v1.20.0/docs/adaptive-exposure.md).
