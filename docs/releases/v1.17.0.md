MidnaFX 1.17.0 adds experimental **Local light response** across visible opaque
scenery. Supported native torch, campfire and stage point lights supply position,
color and influence radius without new asset lists. Select Remaster or the
independent Basic toggle; Advanced strength defaults to 15%, capped at 30%.
Vanilla disables it, and older saved scene looks keep the new feature off.

Up to four supported ordinary lights contribute normal-facing, smooth radial
illumination with eight projected visibility samples. Local and screen-space
color bounce share a bounded energy/headroom envelope and existing fields,
filters and composition. No new rendering pass type, temporal history or host
patch is added. Native lighting and scene alpha remain intact.

This supplements an already lit LDR image. Native lantern/boss spotlights,
effect-light sources, hidden geometry and material-specific relighting remain
outside scope. Four-source selection and sparse screen-space visibility retain
motion/occlusion limits. GPU duration, 4K cost and whole-game art quality remain
unmeasured.

Windows x64 and Intel macOS x86_64 packages are supplied separately. Mac gameplay
remains unverified and support stays best effort through native builds and
portable checks. See [feature and evidence notes](https://github.com/rcreecy/midnafx/blob/v1.17.0/docs/local-lighting.md).
