MidnaFX 1.16.0 improves scene contact lighting across **Ambient occlusion**,
**Contact shadows** and **Screen-space color bounce**. Steep surfaces beside depth
steps retain their surface direction, reducing false contact shading without new
asset lists. Existing Remaster controls and strengths remain unchanged.

The shared half-resolution reconstruction follows projected-plane continuity,
rejects invalid/background neighbors and retains a conservative nearest-depth
fallback near borders or incomplete neighborhoods. Eight analytic fixtures cover
both image axes, reversed depth and odd targets, with normal and rendered-color
assertions against the prior implementation.

No rendering pass, target, temporal history or host patch is added. Up to four
additional depth reads and view reconstructions are required per valid field
pixel. GPU duration and whole-game art/motion quality remain unmeasured. These
remain screen-space effects using visible opaque depth, not new material normals
or a complete lighting replacement.

Windows x64 and Intel macOS x86_64 packages are supplied separately. Mac gameplay
remains unverified and support stays best effort through native builds and
portable checks. See [feature and evidence notes](https://github.com/rcreecy/midnafx/blob/v1.16.0/docs/contact-lighting.md).
