MidnaFX 0.9.1 improves the first-use behavior of the experimental depth-of-field blur.

Live source-matched Windows D3D11 validation showed that the fixed 1200-unit manual
focus default blurred Link and most nearby geometry in the Ordon test scene. Camera-target
autofocus kept Link sharp while separating the foreground and background, so autofocus is
now the default focus source whenever users opt into the still default-off blur. Manual
focus remains available.

The same session visually validated the focus-mask direction, reconstructed-depth view,
HUD exclusion, grading-before-blur order, and resize lifecycle. It also supplied positive
daylight spot checks for Natural / Vivid Realism and the opt-in 110% FOV plus 6-degree lower
camera candidate. Windows build, all 12 tests, shader compilation, and packaging passed.

Depth of field remains experimental. Its fixed blur is strong, foreground silhouette
dilation remains possible, and extended controller-driven motion plus Intel Mac/Metal
runtime validation remain open.

Install the asset matching your platform in Dusklight's mods folder:

- `midnafx-windows-amd64.dusk`
- `midnafx-macos-x86_64.dusk`
