MidnaFX 1.1.0 adds enhanced water to the modern visual stack.

Enhanced water is enabled by default for new installations. It applies
depth-based absorption, animated surface detail, bounded refraction,
Fresnel/environment response, shoreline treatment, and restrained
environment-aware specular. Existing saved water preferences remain unchanged.

The effect uses an exact allowlist of validated static and actor-owned water
surfaces. Unknown, ambiguous, and unsupported water remains native. Waterfalls,
spray, fountains, oil, aquarium glass, particles, and screen-space reflections
are excluded. Water gameplay, collision, buoyancy, animation, splashes, and
authored effects remain game-owned.

Windows source-matched runtime evidence covers outdoor, moving/current, dungeon,
boss, hot-spring, swimming, submerged, shoreline, and waterfall-adjacent scenes.
The package passed Windows build, 15 deterministic tests, Dawn shader validation,
and package-contract validation.

Known limits: the rotating-stair story state and the complete default-on
transition corpus still need a source-matched runtime pass. Intel Mac packages
are CI-built, but live Intel Metal execution and GPU timing are unvalidated.

Install the asset matching your platform in Dusklight's mods folder:

- `midnafx-windows-amd64.dusk`
- `midnafx-macos-x86_64.dusk`
