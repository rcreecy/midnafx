MidnaFX 1.9.0 adds **Contact shadows** across visible opaque scenery and characters.
Short projected rays toward the native environment light add directional
grounding alongside ambient occlusion, without asset or stage lists. Select
**Profile / Remaster** or enable the independent Basic control; strength defaults
to 30%. **Vanilla** disables it. Existing saved settings remain intact until a
profile is selected.

Shadows share AO's half-resolution fields, surface-aware filtering and
reconstruction. AO and shadows retain independent strengths, combined darkening
is capped at 50%, and scene alpha, independent fog and downstream water/HUD order
are preserved. Nearby lights bound the ray correctly after surface bias.

Twenty focused GPU cases cover geometry, direction, range, reversed depth,
filtering, alpha and fog. Native Windows on/off, Remaster resize/reload and normal
close checks pass. This remains a short screen-space approximation: hidden or
off-screen casters, transparent shadows and full per-material lighting are not
provided. Whole-game motion quality and GPU duration remain open.

Windows x64 and Intel macOS x86_64 packages are supplied separately. Mac gameplay
and Metal execution remain unverified; support stays best effort. No game assets
or third-party texture payloads ship. See [feature and evidence notes](https://github.com/rcreecy/midnafx/blob/v1.9.0/docs/contact-shadows.md).
