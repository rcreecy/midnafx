MidnaFX 1.19.0 upgrades **Scene bloom** across bright scenery, characters and
texture replacements. A retained highlight core blends with wider soft halos,
using the existing independent control, 20% default and Remaster integration.
No material lists, new setting or saved-look migration are required.

A bounded pyramid reconstructs up to five levels with normalized tent filters.
Convex merging redistributes glow without multiplying uniform-field brightness.
Highlight headroom, source alpha, dark-scene and zero-strength bypasses remain.
The existing grading/water composite consumes the result; no fullscreen draw or
frame history is added.

At 1080p, bloom uses nine compute dispatches and approximately 2.63 MiB of fields.
At 4K, it uses eleven dispatches and approximately 10.54 MiB, excluding temporary
resize retirement. GPU duration and 4K performance remain unmeasured.

Fourteen GPU cases and portable planning tests cover the production path. A
960×720 synthetic comparison at 20% changes 1,184 additional outer-halo pixels,
while integrated interior field energy differs by 0.32%. Small-highlight movement
retains its energy and measured centroid. These are synthetic checks, not a
whole-game motion or art-quality qualification.

Native desktop capture is unavailable during this iteration. Version 1.19 visual,
reload and normal-close checks remain open. Windows x64 and Intel macOS x86_64
packages are supplied separately; Mac gameplay remains unverified and support
stays best effort through native builds and portable checks. No hosted Metal
execution is required. See [feature and evidence notes](https://github.com/rcreecy/midnafx/blob/v1.19.0/docs/scene-bloom.md).
